About

Bakemon Trading Card Game was released in January 1992 by Dotenin Gaming. At release, 120 cards entered circulation, with planned expansions to follow. The game achieved near-instant popularity with collectors, as some cards became almost mythically difficult to obtain. A complete collection can be sold for a large sum.

Despite official denial from Dotenin Gaming, rumours spread quickly regarding the existence of Secret Cards. Fans of the game have gone to great lengths to uncover these mysterious cards, but each that has been reported so far has been proven to be counterfeit.

This program is a virtual way to collect, store, and battle with Bakemon cards!

Where to Find the Artists

I made this game as a hobby project for five friends. In doing so, I took art from various sources around the internet, without permission, and I edited backgrounds onto them. I did not intend for the game to be played by anyone but my TTRPG group.

Each artist that I could find a source for is listed on the card itself in the bottom right corner. However, I'd like to make it easier for you to find a way to their art. This will be a list of artists I was able to find.

I also used borders and other formatting elements from the official TCG layout. I am not affiliated with The Pokemon Company in any way and do not make any profit off the art or card elements. I am planning to completely remake every card with original (much worse) art and handmade card elements in the future, to truly make Bakemon my own.

Pokemon Sage

A couple of Bakemon evolution lines were taken from the fan-made game Pokemon Sage. I found the art on Pinterest without any credit, and even digging through their wiki, I can't find artist credit for specific Fakemon there. However, it's an awesome, detailed project clearly made by a passionate community, and worth a look.

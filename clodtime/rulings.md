# Bryan's rulings

Rulings Bryan has given when a card could be read two ways. The printed card is law; these
are how to read it. I check here before deciding a report.

- **"Active" usually means "in play."** "While Grupix is active" means while it's anywhere in your
  zone, benched or not. Abilities work from the bench unless a card says otherwise.
- **"Play area" / "play field" means the whole board, both sides.** "Your zone" means your half.
- **The printed card wins over the database.** When they disagree, change the data to match the card.
- **Damage bonuses come from the printed cost.** Weakness and similar bonuses count the energy
  symbols printed on the attack, not the energy that actually paid for it.
- **Thundazolt's Heat Lightning only affects paying for attacks.** Fire and electric can stand in for
  each other when paying a cost. They don't count as each other for damage, and they don't count
  for effects like Lightning Storm's energy count.
- **No damage, no status.** If an attack's damage is completely stopped (Hide, Brigitte, a full
  shield), any status it would put on its target (poison, burn, Hellfire) is lost too. Attacks that
  do no damage at all, only a status (Lullaby, Confusion), still work.
- **Brigitte only covers its wearer.** Anything aimed at the bench gets past the shield: Wǔtái,
  Quake, Swift aimed at the bench, Sporesprinkle's bench damage, Calming Focus. "Unavoidable" damage
  (Swift) gets past it even when aimed at the wearer.
- **A blocked attack still happens; only its target is protected.** When Brigitte blocks, the attack
  still counts as used (Fluff It Up into the shield still sets up Mister Fister). Whatever the
  attacker does on its own side still happens: Chiplum's Swoop still drops energy on its bench. Anything
  that would reach the target doesn't: Voltage Surge's energy for the target stays on Bulbark, because
  the zap never landed.
- **Damage named in the text is that type of damage.** Denticle hits "in fighting damage," so it
  counts as fighting for weakness and for anything that boosts or blocks fighting attacks, even though
  the cost is printed as normal.
- **"They may not add energy" means any way at all.** Silooper's Tail Lasso stops attaching, abilities
  like Soak, and moving energy onto that Bakemon from elsewhere. *(My reading of the printed card,
  Oct 23. Not confirmed by Bryan yet.)*
- **Dice damage is always in tens.** Damage only moves in units of 10. A D6 or D3 "of damage" (or of
  damage reduction, like Starfall) means 10–60 or 10–30.
- **Parrot copies only the attack its target made on the opponent's most recent turn.** If that
  Bakemon was knocked out or replaced, or the opponent didn't attack, there's nothing to copy.
- **Weather belongs to the whole table, but remembers who called it.** Only one weather at a time, and
  anyone can replace it. Its effect can still single out one side ("your opponent's Bakemon…"), written
  from the summoner's point of view. Frost already works this way. (Bryan, Oct 24.)
- **Fire Watch lasts five of the opponent's turns.** It burns their active Bakemon at the start of each,
  and anything they send into the active slot meanwhile. (Bryan, Oct 24.)
- **Acid Rain only stops when its side drops below five dark energy, or Leapek leaves.** If it's cleared
  or replaced, it falls again on Leapek's side's next turn. Only that side can use it, and the caller
  chooses which energy turns dark. (Bryan, Oct 24.)
- **Weather names:** Draquaduct's weather is Flood. Crackazolt's Arclight brings Lightningshower (renamed
  so it isn't confused with Thundazolt's Heat Lightning ability). No weather at all is Blue Sky.
- **Iveldyr's Spore Bloom lands after four turns**, at the start of Iveldyr's side's turn. The caster
  chooses which mushroom Bakemon share the 60 HP of healing.
- **Practice mode reshuffles the discard pile when the deck runs out.** At the table you can discard
  any time, so you'd get there anyway. The rules page stays as written for human matches.
- **Humidifier only helps attacks.** Its extra water energy isn't a real energy card. It counts toward
  paying for attacks, but not for retreating or anything else that spends energy.
- **Only the printed symbols decide an attack's type.** Humidifier's extra water doesn't make an attack
  water-type: no water weakness, and Umbrella doesn't block it. Paying a colorless cost with water
  doesn't make it a water attack either.
- **Retreating to the bench clears statuses.** Buffs stay for now. Once the weather system is
  finished, only buffs that give the table its weather should stay when a Bakemon retreats.
- **"Choose any target in the play area" means either side, and the attack goes to that target.**
  Kidding's Mischief can pick one of your own Bakemon (to eat its grass); the 10 damage goes there too.
  (Bryan, Oct 24.)
- **Weaknesses aren't relics.** Shrizzle and Lobscorch are weak to fighting on purpose. The fighting →
  ground change only applied to cards Bryan actually changed. Go by the printed card.
- **The bots don't need to be optimal.** As long as a move is possible, Nyx not choosing it is fine.
- **An "x" after damage means "times something", and the card's text says what.** Sporesprinkle's
  10x is 10 for each Bakemon it hits; on other cards it can be 10 for each hit or each flip. It's
  never a leftover. (Bryan, Oct 31.)
- **Deck limits by rarity:** common, uncommon and rare: 3 copies. Double rare: 2. Ultra rare: 1.
  Legendary: one legendary card per deck, except a legendary line counts as one (one of each card in
  it; today only Eclidyr → Ionodyr). The Battle Standard button fills a collection to these numbers.
  (Bryan, Oct 31.)

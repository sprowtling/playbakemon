# Bryan's rulings

Rulings Bryan has given when a card could be read two ways. The printed card is law; these
are how to read it. I check here before deciding a report.

- **"Active" usually means "in play."** "While Grupix is active" means while it's anywhere in your
  zone, benched or not. Abilities work from the bench unless a card says otherwise.
- **"Play area" / "play field" means the whole board, both sides.** "Your zone" means your half.
- **The printed card wins over the database.** When they disagree, change the data to match the card.
- **Damage bonuses come from the printed cost.** Weakness and similar bonuses count the energy
  symbols printed on the attack, not the energy that actually paid for it.
- **Thundazolt's Heat Lightning only affects paying for attacks.** Fire and electric can stand in for
  each other when paying a cost. They don't count as each other for damage, and they don't count
  for effects like Lightning Storm's energy count.
- **No damage, no burn.** An attack that does no damage (Hide, a full shield) can't inflict a status
  that rides on the hit, such as Hellfire's burn.
- **Iveldyr's Spore Bloom lands after four turns**, at the start of Iveldyr's side's turn. The caster
  chooses which mushroom Bakemon share the 60 HP of healing.
- **Practice mode reshuffles the discard pile when the deck runs out.** At the table you can discard
  any time, so you'd get there anyway. The rules page stays as written for human matches.
- **Weaknesses aren't relics.** Shrizzle and Lobscorch are weak to fighting on purpose. The fighting →
  ground change only applied to cards Bryan actually changed. Go by the printed card.
- **The bots don't need to be optimal.** As long as a move is possible, Nyx not choosing it is fine.
